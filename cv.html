Projects Completed

2026 – 2027
Impact Analysis of EV Charging Infrastructure at IIT Kanpur

Assessing the impact of EV charging infrastructure on the IIT Kanpur distribution network through time-series power-flow analysis, EV charging-demand modelling, and evaluation of voltage deviations, transformer loading, and network hosting capacity.

2025 – 2026
Renewable Energy Integrated Smart Charging Framework

Developed an integrated simulation framework combining renewable energy sources, battery energy storage systems (BESS), energy management systems (EMS), distribution operators, and EV charging infrastructure for smart grid applications.

Distribution Grid Digital Twin using OpenDSS

Integrated EV charging digital twin with IEEE distribution feeder models in OpenDSS to evaluate voltage regulation, transformer loading, and hosting capacity under different EV penetration scenarios using Monte Carlo simulations.

2024 – 2025
AI-Based Charging Power Prediction

Developed deep learning models (LSTM and hybrid machine learning approaches) to predict active and reactive charging power directly from battery State-of-Charge (SOC). Built automated preprocessing, feature engineering, and inference pipelines using Python and TensorFlow.

2022 – 2023
Real-Time EV Charging Data Acquisition using µPMU

Designed an experimental data acquisition platform integrating a Siemens CPC50 DC fast charger, TATA Nexon EV, and micro-Phasor Measurement Unit (µPMU). Collected synchronized high-resolution voltage, current, active power, reactive power, and SOC datasets for digital twin development.

2021 – 2022
Life Cycle Assessment and Sustainability Analysis of Electric Vehicles

Developed a GREET-based life cycle assessment framework to evaluate greenhouse gas emissions of electric and conventional vehicles under Indian operating conditions for cradle-to-gate (CTG) and well-to-wheel (WTW). Performed vehicle manufacturing, battery production, and fuel-cycle analysis to support sustainable transportation planning and decarbonization strategies.
