Annapurna Base Camp Trek

One of the most famous treks in the world. It takes you into the mighty Himalayas and to the base of the Annapurna Mountains (4,100 m / 13,450 ft), beneath Annapurna I at 8,091 m, one of the fourteen 8000 m peaks. On the way you pass traditional villages, bamboo forests, waterfalls, glaciers, hot springs and suspension bridges.

Minkiani Pass Trek

Situated at 4,343 m in the Kangra district of Himachal Pradesh, this is one of the least explored treks in the state. Ahead of Dharamshala on the Dhauladhar range, it offers views of the Pirpanjal and Zanskar ranges to the north, and the trail passes through the popular Kareri Lake.
